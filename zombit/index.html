About Zombit

Zombit is a day-and-night zombie survival game with a compact pixel-art world. Daytime is for chopping, gathering, and improving the base; nighttime turns those preparations into a direct test against attacking zombies.

The game opens directly in the player above, so you can start on horror-games.github.io without installing a separate app.

How to Play

Gather wood and other materials while the area is safe. Build or repair the shelter before nightfall, keep a weapon ready, and return to damaged structures after every attack so the next night does not begin with a weak base.

Quick Tips

  • Use every daylight cycle efficiently.
  • Repair the base before adding extras.
  • Keep materials for emergency fixes.
  • Fight near defenses instead of far away.
