About Haunted Dorm

Haunted Dorm turns a simple bedroom into a compact survival base. Your bed generates resources, the door keeps danger outside, and every turret or upgrade buys a little more time against an increasingly powerful hunter.

The game opens directly in the player above, so you can start on horror-games.github.io without installing a separate app.

How to Play

Choose an open room quickly and start upgrading the bed so it produces more resources. Reinforce the door before pressure builds, then add turrets and support items. Balance income and defense instead of spending everything on one upgrade.

Quick Tips

  • Claim a room quickly at the start.
  • Upgrade income before costs rise.
  • Repair and reinforce the door early.
  • Add several turrets for steady damage.
