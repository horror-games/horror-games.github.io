About Zombie Survivors

Zombie Survivors is a fast horde-survival game where attacks fire automatically and movement is your main defense. Each level offers new weapons or upgrades, letting every run grow from a weak start into a chaotic survival build.

The game opens directly in the player above, so you can start on horror-games.github.io without installing a separate app.

How to Play

Move around the edges of the horde and collect experience without letting enemies close every escape route. Choose upgrades that work together, then look for weapon combinations that improve damage, range, or crowd control.

Quick Tips

  • Circle the horde instead of standing still.
  • Collect experience before it spreads out.
  • Combine weapons with matching upgrades.
  • Leave yourself a clear escape path.
