About TDS Tower Destiny Survive

TDS Tower Destiny Survive combines zombie combat with vehicle construction. Every crate changes the shape of your tower, while mounted weapons, character gear, health, damage, and energy upgrades decide how far the build can travel.

The game opens directly in the player above, so you can start on horror-games.github.io without installing a separate app.

How to Play

Collect resources without ignoring incoming enemies, then place crates and weapons so both sides of the platform are protected. Upgrade the weakest part of the build and prepare for bosses before moving into a new area.

Quick Tips

  • Balance tower height with health.
  • Cover both sides with mounted weapons.
  • Keep enough energy for key equipment.
  • Save strong upgrades for boss encounters.
