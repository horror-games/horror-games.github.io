About Zombie Knocks Door 3D

Zombie Knocks Door 3D mixes idle income with room-defense strategy. A bed produces the resources you need, while door upgrades and defensive equipment determine whether your shelter survives each stronger attack.

The game opens directly in the player above, so you can start on horror-games.github.io without installing a separate app.

How to Play

Choose a room, begin producing resources, and upgrade the door before the zombie reaches full strength. Add weapons after the entrance is stable, then keep income, repairs, and damage growing at a similar pace.

Quick Tips

  • Start generating resources immediately.
  • Do not leave the door at its base level.
  • Repair before the next heavy attack.
  • Balance income with defensive damage.
